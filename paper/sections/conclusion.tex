\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \textbf{\fedhep{}}, an efficient single-backbone federated learning framework coordinating three representation tiers---global consensus, collaborative peer clusters, and private local heads---to balance statistical heterogeneity, edge memory constraints, and Byzantine vulnerability. Pairing Active-Class Logit Masking (\textbf{ACLM}) with skew-calibrated loss weighting suppresses gradient interference from absent categories without dual-model memory overhead. Low-dimensional Random Projections discover peer clusters while preserving edge privacy, and Subspace-Constrained Cosine Filtering (\textbf{SCCF}) with Temporal Trust Tracking (\textbf{TTT}) defends against Byzantine poisoning while mitigating false-positive penalties on specialized nodes. Benchmarks on CIFAR-100 and FEMNIST, 50-client scaling, and MobileNetV3 edge profiling demonstrate improved personalization accuracy, tail fairness, and single-model efficiency. Future work will explore formal differential privacy (DP) bounds for sketches and asynchronous edge participation.
